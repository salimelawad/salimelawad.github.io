Suunly™ Time

Suunly™ time is based on the position of the sun in the sky. Unlike standard time, which uses fixed time zones, Suunly™ time varies based on your exact location on Earth.

Key Features:

  • Suunly™ noon (12:00) occurs when the sun is directly overhead.
  • Each degree of longitude equals 4 minutes of Suunly™ time difference.
  • Suunly™ time varies throughout the year due to Earth's elliptical orbit.
  • No daylight saving time adjustments needed.

This application calculates Suunly™ time based on your location's latitude and longitude, taking into account the equation of time to provide accurate Suunly™ time readings.

Housing Price Analysis App

An interactive web application that analyzes U.S. housing price trends, adjusting for inflation and the impact of mortgage rates to provide a comprehensive view of the real cost of buying a home.

Summary of Write-up:

The accompanying article, "Priced Out: Why Homes Are More Expensive Than Ever Before," delves into how factors like inflation and mortgage rates contribute to current housing affordability challenges. It reveals that housing is more expensive now in real terms than ever before by examining the Case-Shiller Home Price Index and mortgage payment indexes.

Key Features:

  • Inflation-adjusted Case-Shiller Home Price Index.
  • Analysis based on 30-year and 15-year fixed mortgage rates.
  • Tracks and compares housing prices across U.S. cities.
  • Built with Streamlit and Python.
